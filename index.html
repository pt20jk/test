Travel & Access to Iksan

Iksan Station is the central high-speed KTX/SRT railway hub in western South Korea

🚄 KTX / SRT Express Rail

Direct high-speed trains connection from Seoul (Yongsan/Suseo Station) to Iksan Station in ~70 mins. Train runs every 20-30 minutes.

✈️ From Incheon Int'l Airport

Take AREX Express train to Yongsan Station, then transfer directly to KTX. Or take direct Limousine Airport Bus to Iksan Terminal (~3 hrs).

🚌 Iksan City Tour Bus

Specially operated weekend routes connecting Iksan KTX Station to UNESCO sites, Prison Set & Gem Museum for only KRW 2,000 per day pass!
